TEXAS HOLD 'EM is a poker game in which each player gets two pocket cards, while five community cards are dealt face up on the table.

--- Types of Cards --- 

COMMUNITY CARDS refer to the cards placed on the table after betting.
POCKET CARDS refer to the cards the player is dealt at the beginning of every round.
FLOP refers to the first three COMMUNITY CARDS that are placed in TEXAS HOLD 'EM.
TURN refers to the fourth COMMUNITY CARD and the sixth card dealt in TEXAS HOLD 'EM.
RIVER refers to the fifth COMMUNITY CARD, the seventh and final card dealt in TEXAS HOLD 'EM.

--- Player Choices ---

FOLD is when a player chooses to abandon their hand to the deck.
RAISE is to increase the the amount of the bet (if a bet has been opened).
ALL-IN is when a player chooses to bet all their money.
CHECK is a decision a player can make if no one has decided to open a bet before this point. Akin to calling a bet of £0.
CALL is to match the current bet. 

--- Bets ---

BLINDS is a forced bet in TEXAS HOLD 'EM that must be posted before you see any cards. There are two types of blinds.
BIG BLINDS are usually 1/100th of the starting stack. However, here it is 1/10th.
SMALL BLINDS are usually 1/2 of BIG BLINDS. The player left of the dealer will place a SMALL BLIND. Everyone else must put match, or raise the BIG BLIND. 
    Since this mode only has two players, Players 1 and 2 will take turn placing the SMALL BLIND or BIG BLIND  

--- Hand Rankings, sorted from highest to lowest --- 

ROYAL FLUSH consists of an ace, king, queen, jack, and 10 of the same suit. 
STRAIGHT FLUSH consists of five consecutive cards that are of the same suit.
FOUR-OF-A-KIND consists of four cards of the same value.
FULL HOUSE consists of a THREE-OF-A-KIND with a PAIR of a different value.
FLUSH consists of five cards of the same suit, but not in a consecutive order.
STRAIGHT consists of five consecutive cards of the same suit. 
THREE-OF-A-KIND consists of three cards of the same value.
TWO PAIR consists of two pairs of cards.
ONE PAIR consists of two cards of the same value.
HIGH CARD only consists of one high ranking card, when the five cards do not produce any of the above.

--- Stages ---
SHOWDOWN occurs at the end of every round where players reveal their cards and the winner is determined.
PRE-FLOP is the stage of the game that takes place after the players receive their POCKET CARDS before the FLOP.
FLOP is the stage of the game that refers to the first three community cards dealt face-up on the table after the PRE-FLOP betting round
